## Severity Levels

| Level | Definition | Response SLA |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| Critical | Active breach, data loss, full service compromise | < 1 hour |
| High | Suspected breach, compromised credentials | < 4 hours |
| Medium | Misconfiguration, failed intrusion attempt | < 24 hours |
| Low | Anomalous activity, minor policy violation | < 1 week |

## Process

1. Incident detected → logged here within 1 hour
2. Postmortem completed within 48 hours of resolution
3. Postmortem document linked in the **Postmortem** column above
4. Register reviewed semi-annually by CTO
